Privacy Policy for Ad Stripper

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Collection

Ad Stripper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data. We do not track your browsing history.

2. Local Storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local solely to save your settings (e.g., whether ad blocking is enabled) and your premium license status on your own device. This data never leaves your browser.

3. Permissions

The extension requires access to *.youtube.com only to inject scripts that remove advertisements. It does not run on any other websites.

4. Third-Party Services

For premium activation, users manually submit a cryptocurrency transaction hash (TxID). This hash is sent to our verification server solely to confirm payment. It is not linked to your identity.

5. Contact

If you have questions, contact us at support@farm-store.ru.
